# Basis: <stage name>

One or two sentences: what a skill built from this basis does, what it
receives as input, and what it hands over when it is done.

Out of scope: what belongs to another stage.

## Facts

How Odoo behaves in this area. True whatever the workflow is. Checkable
against code. Mark anything that changes between versions.

## Fixed rules

Team or Odoo policy. Each rule is followed by its reason.

## Decisions

Questions the agent asks the person. Each one has:

- the question, phrased as it should be asked;
- why the answer changes the skill;
- a default for people who have no preference.

## Your material

What the person should bring: real tickets, commands, recurring corrections.

## Test prompts

How to check the built skill: what to give it and what a good result is.
